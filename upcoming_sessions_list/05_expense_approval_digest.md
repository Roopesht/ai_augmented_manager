# Session 5: Expense & Reimbursement Approval Digest

**Manager problem:** Expense claims arrive as ad-hoc emails and Teams messages with receipts attached; approving them means digging through channels and manually checking policy limits.

**Channels:** Outlook mail (claims + receipt attachments), Teams (approval pings), Excel `policy.xlsx` (category limits), Excel `people.xlsx` (project/cost-center mapping).

**Magic:** Summarize all pending claims in one digest, flag anything breaching `policy.xlsx` limits, and draft approve/query responses. Catches the trend of a person or category consistently running over policy.

**Data:** Separate dataset — expense-claim emails with attachments, varying categories (travel, client entertainment, supplies), a couple of deliberate policy-breach cases, and `policy.xlsx`.

**Outcome:** Demonstrates handling attachments and policy-based flagging — the first step from read-only reporting toward semi-automated action.
