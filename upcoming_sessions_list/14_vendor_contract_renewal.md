# Session 14: Vendor & Contract Renewal Tracker

**Manager problem:** Vendor contracts auto-renew or lapse because no one is tracking expiry dates against active negotiation status.

**Channels:** Outlook mail (vendor correspondence), Outlook Calendar (negotiation meetings), Excel `contracts.xlsx` (expiry dates, contract value, auto-renewal terms).

**Magic:** Cross-check ongoing correspondence against `contracts.xlsx` expiry dates, catch the trend of a renewal deadline approaching with no active negotiation thread, and summarize which contracts are at risk of silent auto-renewal or lapse. Next steps (schedule negotiation, request better terms) get tracked to closure.

**Data:** Separate dataset — vendor email threads at varying negotiation stages, plus `contracts.xlsx` with a mix of near-term and distant expiry dates.

**Outcome:** No contract renews or lapses by accident again.
