# Session 4: Attrition & Notice Period Tracker

**Manager problem:** Resignation signals are scattered — formal notice in email, disengagement visible only in Teams/Slack activity and 1:1 calendar patterns — so risk is spotted too late.

**Channels:** Outlook mail (resignation/HR threads), Outlook Calendar (1:1 cadence, exit interviews), Teams/Slack (declining activity/sentiment), Excel `people.xlsx` (org chart, project assignment, rate for backfill cost).

**Magic:** Catch the early trend (reduced Slack/Teams activity, skipped 1:1s) before a formal resignation lands, summarize attrition risk per project against `people.xlsx`, and track backfill next-steps status (requisition raised, interviews scheduled) over time.

**Data:** Separate dataset — resignation emails plus matching calendar/activity patterns for a subset of the team.

**Outcome:** An attrition risk view built from behavioral signal, not just HR paperwork, tying back to the Cost vs Revenue story from Session 1.
