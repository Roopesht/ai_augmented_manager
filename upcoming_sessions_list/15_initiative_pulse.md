# Session 15: Initiative Pulse — Cross-Channel Trend & Next-Steps Journal (Capstone)

**Manager problem:** No single place answers "how is this initiative actually going" — the truth is split across mail, calendar, Teams, Slack, and a spreadsheet nobody updates.

**Channels:** Outlook mail, Outlook Calendar, Teams, Slack, CRM/account details, Excel `projects.xlsx` (expected outcomes, milestones) — everything from Sessions 1-14 feeding one view.

**Magic:** Ingest all channel activity tied to one initiative, reconcile it against the expected outcome in `projects.xlsx`, catch the trend (momentum slowing, cadence dropping, sentiment cooling), auto-summarize current state versus plan, identify next steps, and maintain a running journal that shows whether last cycle's next steps actually happened.

**Data:** Reuses every dataset from Sessions 1-14 for one initiative, tied together.

**Outcome:** The single "state of the initiative" view every manager wishes existed — generated automatically, not compiled by hand. Best closing session for the workshop.
