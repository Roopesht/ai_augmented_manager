# Session 3: Cross-Channel Next-Steps Tracker

**Manager problem:** Action items get scattered across meeting emails, Teams threads, and Slack DMs — no single place tracks who owns what or what's stalling.

**Channels:** Teams chat, Slack, Outlook mail (meeting-minute emails), Outlook Calendar (recurring meeting series), Excel `people.xlsx` (owner lookup).

**Magic:** Pull next-steps wherever they're mentioned, dedupe the same item appearing in Slack and email, and track each one's status across weeks. Catch the trend of items that keep reappearing unresolved across multiple meetings — a signal a topic is quietly stalling — and flag it before it becomes a real problem.

**Data:** Separate dataset — meeting-minute emails plus matching Teams/Slack snippets referencing the same items with varied phrasing.

**Outcome:** One always-current next-steps ledger, with a built-in "this has been open for 3 weeks" alert, no separate task tool needed.
