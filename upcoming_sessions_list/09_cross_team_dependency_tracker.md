# Session 9: Cross-Team Dependency Tracker

**Manager problem:** A deliverable is blocked on another team, but that only becomes visible after the deadline is missed.

**Channels:** Teams/Slack (cross-team channels), Outlook mail (handoff threads), Excel `projects.xlsx` (dependency map between workstreams).

**Magic:** Match chatter across teams to the dependency map, catch the trend of a dependency's status not moving across check-ins, and summarize which initiatives are genuinely blocked versus just quiet. Next steps get assigned to the owning team and tracked to closure.

**Data:** Separate dataset — cross-team channel messages referencing shared deliverables, some progressing, some stalled, plus `projects.xlsx`.

**Outcome:** Blocked dependencies surface while there's still time to unblock them, not after the deadline slips.
