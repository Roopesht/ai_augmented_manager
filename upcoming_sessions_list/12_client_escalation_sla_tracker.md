# Session 12: Client Escalation & SLA Tracker

**Manager problem:** Escalations arrive across support email, incident channels, and urgent calls — with no unified view of SLA exposure until a client complains.

**Channels:** Outlook mail (support threads), Teams/Slack (incident channels), Outlook Calendar (escalation calls), Excel `sla.xlsx` (response/resolution thresholds per account).

**Magic:** Match incidents across channels to `sla.xlsx` thresholds, catch the trend of rising escalation volume or breach risk for an account, summarize each incident, and track resolution next-steps status until closed.

**Data:** Separate dataset — incident/support threads across email and chat for a few accounts, some within SLA, some breaching, plus `sla.xlsx`.

**Outcome:** SLA breaches are visible while they're still preventable, not after the client escalates further.
