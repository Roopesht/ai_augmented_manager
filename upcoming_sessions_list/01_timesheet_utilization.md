# Session 1: Timesheet & Utilization Compliance

**Manager problem:** Chasing missing timesheets and manually reconciling them against actual work to know real utilization.

**Channels:** Outlook mail (timesheet submissions), Outlook Calendar (actual hours booked in meetings/focus blocks), Excel knowledge base — `people.xlsx` (roles, billable rate, project assignment), `projects.xlsx` (planned effort per project).

**Magic:** Cross-check calendar-booked time vs submitted timesheet vs planned effort. Catch the trend (whose compliance/utilization is slipping week over week), summarize per-project utilization against plan, and surface next steps ("chase Rohan, re-baseline Project X effort") — tracked so next week shows whether they were actioned.

**Data:** Separate dataset — new Outlook-style timesheet emails/calendar events, plus `people.xlsx`/`projects.xlsx` as the ground-truth knowledge layer.

**Outcome:** A living utilization view reconciled across three sources instead of one manual spreadsheet.
