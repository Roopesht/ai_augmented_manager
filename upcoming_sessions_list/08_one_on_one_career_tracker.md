# Session 8: 1:1 & Career Conversation Tracker

**Manager problem:** Commitments made in 1:1s (training, stretch project, promotion conversation) get forgotten by the next cycle.

**Channels:** Outlook Calendar (1:1 cadence), Outlook mail/Teams (post-1:1 notes), Excel `people.xlsx` (career goals, skip-level notes).

**Magic:** Extract commitments from 1:1 notes, track them against `people.xlsx`, and flag ones open past a reasonable window ("training promised 6 weeks ago, not yet scheduled"). Catches the trend of a manager consistently under-following-through with one report versus another.

**Data:** Separate dataset — 1:1 notes with embedded commitments of varying follow-through.

**Outcome:** Turns easy-to-forget career promises into a tracked, fair, auditable trail — useful for both the manager and performance calibration.
