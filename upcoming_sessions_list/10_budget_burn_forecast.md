# Session 10: Budget Burn & Forecast Tracker

**Manager problem:** Budget overruns are discovered at month-end, not while there's still time to course-correct.

**Channels:** Outlook mail (PO/invoice approvals), Teams (spend discussions), Excel `budget.xlsx` (plan vs actuals per project).

**Magic:** Reconcile approved spend from email/Teams against `budget.xlsx`, catch the burn-rate trend versus forecast, and summarize which initiatives are on track versus heading over. Next steps (renegotiate scope, request more budget, pause spend) get tracked to see if they were acted on.

**Data:** Separate dataset — invoice/PO approval emails and `budget.xlsx` with a couple of projects trending over plan.

**Outcome:** A financial early-warning system built from correspondence a manager already receives, not a separate finance report.
