# Session 13: Meeting Load & Focus-Time Health

**Manager problem:** The team is "busy" but burnt out — too many meetings, no protected focus time — and it's invisible until someone flags it.

**Channels:** Outlook Calendar (meeting density per person), Excel `people.xlsx` (role, expected focus-time norms).

**Magic:** Compute meeting load and fragmented-time per person, catch the trend of it worsening over weeks, and summarize which individuals or teams are over threshold. Next steps ("decline optional recurring sync", "block focus mornings") get suggested and tracked for adoption.

**Data:** Separate dataset — calendar exports for the team with varying meeting density, some improving after intervention, some not.

**Outcome:** A concrete, trend-based case for protecting focus time instead of a vague "we're too busy" complaint.
