# Session 6: Client Health & Renewal Risk Tracker

**Manager problem:** No single view of whether a client relationship is healthy until a renewal is already at risk.

**Channels:** CRM/account details, Outlook mail (client correspondence cadence), Teams/Slack (client-facing channel sentiment), Outlook Calendar (meeting frequency), Excel `accounts.xlsx` (contract value, renewal date).

**Magic:** Catch the trend — slowing email/meeting cadence, cooling sentiment — well before the renewal date in `accounts.xlsx`. Summarize account health, identify next steps (executive check-in, QBR), and track whether they happened.

**Data:** Separate dataset — a handful of client accounts with declining and healthy engagement patterns across channels, plus `accounts.xlsx`.

**Outcome:** An early-warning account health signal instead of a renewal surprise.
